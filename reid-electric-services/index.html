Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?

Frequent breaker trips, flickering or dimming lights when appliances kick on, a panel over 20–25 years old, a fuse box instead of breakers, or adding a major load like an EV charger or hot tub are common signs. We can evaluate your panel's capacity and code compliance on-site.

Is a flickering light an emergency?

Occasional flicker in one fixture is often just a loose bulb or connection. Flickering across multiple rooms, especially paired with a burning smell or warm outlets, should be treated as urgent — call us right away.

What's the difference between a fuse box and a breaker panel?

Fuses are one-time-use and must be physically replaced when blown. Breakers trip and can simply be reset, and modern panels support AFCI/GFCI protection that fuse boxes can't provide.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, we offer free quotes on our services.

Can I add an EV charger to my existing home?

Usually yes, but your panel's available capacity and circuit routing need to be evaluated first. Some homes need a panel upgrade to support a Level 2 charger — we handle that evaluation, permitting, and installation.

Do I need a permit for electrical work?

Most panel upgrades, rewiring, and new circuits require a permit and inspection. We handle that as part of the job.

What size generator do I need?

It depends on which circuits you want backed up — just the essentials or the whole house. A proper load calculation determines the right size, and standby generators require a transfer switch so power never backfeeds into utility lines.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes — regular hours are Monday–Friday, 7:00AM–3:30PM, but we take on-call jobs after hours and on weekends when the situation calls for it. Free quotes either way.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Central Alabama and primarily serve the Birmingham metro area, with extended service into Georgia and Tennessee for larger projects, generally within about a 3-hour drive depending on job size.
